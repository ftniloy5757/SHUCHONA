3. COMPLETE 18-MODULE FUNCTIONAL SPECIFICATION
====================================================================

### MODULE 1: Authentication & Audio-Visual Informed Consent
- Clean onboarding with Phone OTP or Email sign-up (plus a one-tap "Continue in Offline Demo Mode" option for instant testing).
- Mandatory **Informed Data Consent Screen** before any user profile or health data is saved: explains in plain Bangla and English how data is kept private and anonymized, featuring an auto-playable **"🔊 Listen to Consent"** button and an explicit **"I Agree & Continue"** action.

### MODULE 2, 3 & 4: Multilingual Safety-Aligned AI Chatbot (Voice STT + TTS)
- Chat interface supporting both **Push-to-Talk Bangla/English Voice Input** (with live waveform indicator) and text input.
- Every assistant message renders as a structured card with a **🔊 Read Aloud** button.
- System prompt enforces warm, non-stigmatizing, low-literacy Bangla/English responses grounded in WHO and DGHS Bangladesh health education guidelines, strictly prohibiting medical diagnoses or new prescriptions.

### MODULE 5 & 6: Women’s Health Symptom Guide & Deterministic Red-Flag Engine
- Guided voice or icon-assisted symptom intake covering irregular periods, severe menstrual cramps, hormonal/PCOS indicators, breast lump or pain, and general reproductive wellness.
- **Tier 1 Deterministic On-Device Red-Flag Gatekeeper:** Runs *before* any LLM API call. Scans Bangla and English inputs for red-flag keywords (e.g., severe heavy bleeding/hemorrhage, fainting, hard/fixed breast lump with skin dimpling or nipple discharge, pregnancy bleeding, self-harm). If matched, immediately displays a high-priority crimson **Urgent Warning Card** with spoken Bangla alert: *"জরুরি সতর্কতা: অনুগ্রহ করে দেরি না করে দ্রুত নিকটস্থ হাসপাতাল বা ডাক্তারের পরামর্শ নিন"* and a one-tap **Call 16263 / Find Clinic** button.
- **Tier 2 Grounded Symptom Exploration:** Asks 2–3 structured follow-up questions, explains in simple language which common conditions (e.g., PCOS, PMS, benign vs. concerning breast changes) the symptom patterns may relate to, provides a transparent **"Why This Guidance?"** explanation pill, and permanently pins the mandatory disclaimer on every result:
  - English: *"This is AI guidance only, not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a doctor."*
  - Bangla: *"এটি শুধুমাত্র AI-ভিত্তিক প্রাথমিক তথ্য, কোনো চিকিৎসা নির্ণয় নয়। অনুগ্রহ করে একজন ডাক্তারের পরামর্শ নিন।"*

### MODULE 7: PrescribeSafe BD — Handwritten Prescription & Generic Drug Guard
- Camera/gallery capture flow where users snap a photo of a handwritten Bangladeshi prescription (include 2 realistic sample Bangladeshi prescription test cases in assets so judges can test with one tap even without a physical paper).
- **Multimodal Vision + DGDA/DIMS RAG Pipeline:**
  1. Extracts prescribed medications, strengths, durations, and local shorthand (`1+0+1`, `আহারের পর / খাওয়ার আগে`).
  2. Matches extracted drugs against a bundled local **DGDA/DIMS Bangladesh Drug Dataset** (minimum 35 common Bangladeshi brands & generics with real BDT ৳ unit prices, active ingredients, and interaction rules).
  3. **Confidence Gate:** If OCR confidence for a medication is `< 85%`, displays an amber **"Verify with Local Pharmacist"** warning badge rather than guessing.
  4. **Drug-Drug Interaction (DDI) & Duplicate Ingredient Check:** Flags dangerous multi-doctor combinations or duplicate active ingredients in red with clear Bangla audio explanations.
  5. **Generic Price Savings Card:** Displays cheaper DGDA-approved local generic equivalents side-by-side with exact BDT (৳) savings percentage.
  6. **One-Tap Bangla Voice Dosage Scheduler:** Converts the extracted schedule (`1+0+1`) into scheduled local notifications that speak the exact instruction in Bangla (`"সকালের ওষুধ খাওয়ার সময় হয়েছে — ১টি ট্যাবলেট খাওয়ার পরে"`).

### MODULE 8 & 9: Habit Tracker & Privacy-Safe Habit Improvement Leaderboard
- **Habit Tracker:** Visual icon grid to build healthy habits (Drink 8 Glasses Water, Iron/Folic Acid Tablet, 20-Min Walk, Sleep Before 11 PM) or quit harmful habits (Betel Nut/Jarda, Smoking, Excess Sugar). Features one-tap daily check-ins, animated streak counters, and offline caching.
- **Habit Improvement Leaderboard:** Privacy-preserving leaderboard where users appear under selectable nature/floral avatars and pseudonyms (or compete as a village Self-Help Group cohort). Ranked by **Weekly Improvement XP (% streak growth)** rather than raw totals so newcomers can win, without revealing which specific habit a user is tracking.

### MODULE 10: Geofenced Blood Demand Alert Network
- Users opt in with their blood group (`A+, A-, B+, B-, O+, O-, AB+, AB-`, or `Unknown - Find Free Test`), Upazila/District, and last donation date (enforcing a 90-day medical cooldown).
- **One-Tap Blood SOS Broadcast:** Users or community health workers can post an urgent blood request (Blood Group, Units Needed, Hospital Name, Urgency Level). Filters eligible out-of-cooldown donors in the same Upazila, triggers a high-priority Bangla voice/push alert simulation, and tracks donor responses (`I Can Donate` / `Share`).

### MODULE 11: One-Tap Craving & Crisis Support Mode
- Prominent **"আমি কষ্টে আছি / I’m Struggling"** emergency button on the home dashboard.
- Opens an immediate calming full-screen modal featuring an interactive **4-4-6 Guided Breathing Animation** with spoken Bangla audio pacing, 3 step-by-step grounding/coping cards, and a direct one-tap fallback button to call a live human counselor / national helpline (`16263` / `Kaan Pete Roi`).

### MODULE 12 & 13: Mental Wellness Tips, Weekly Mood Analytics & Personalized Guidance
- Daily voice-enabled Bangla/English mental wellness micro-tip card.
- 5-emoji daily mood check-in (`খুব ভালো` to `খুব খারাপ`) visualized on a clean `fl_chart` 7-day mood & habit consistency chart.
- **Context-Aware Personalized Guidance:** Rule-and-AI engine on the home screen that generates tailored daily nudges based on the user's recent mood dips, missed habit streaks, or active PrescribeSafe medication schedules.

### MODULE 14, 15, 16 & 17: Find Care Directory, Offline Sync, Low-Literacy UI & Smart Reminders
- **Find Care Info:** Offline-accessible directory of nearby Upazila Health Complexes, Community Clinics, Women's Health Centers, and National Helplines with distance tags, spoken Bangla directions summary, and one-tap Call buttons.
- **Offline Sync Indicator:** Subtle status pill in the header (`অনলাইন / সিঙ্ক সম্পন্ন` vs. `অফলাইন মোড — তথ্য সংরক্ষিত আছে`) demonstrating real-time offline queueing and sync.
- **Smart Local Reminders:** Customizable morning/evening voice-assisted notification triggers.

### MODULE 18: Privacy-Preserving Admin Impact Dashboard
- Accessible via a role switcher in Settings/Header (`Community User View` ↔ `NGO / Admin Impact Dashboard`).
- Displays strictly anonymized, PII-stripped population health analytics using `fl_chart`:
  - Total Active Self-Help Group Members & Offline Check-ins
  - Top Anonymized Symptom Clusters by Upazila (e.g., PCOS/Cycle Irregularity, Anemia/Fatigue)
  - Total Estimated Patient Medication Savings (in BDT ৳) via PrescribeSafe BD generic substitutions
  - Blood Demand Alert Fulfillment Rate & Average Donor Response Time
  - Explicit badge confirming: *"Zero PII Stored — All Identifiers Stripped On-Device Before Aggregation."*
